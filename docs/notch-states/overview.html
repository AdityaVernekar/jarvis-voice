1. Resting notch

Active count on the left, status on the right, with the MacBook camera area kept clear in the centre.

2. Agents list

list

Click the notch to see your agents and their status. Click a row to go to that agent.

3. Permission request

open

Review a tool request and choose Allow, Deny or In terminal. Always allow appears only when offered.

open
